Create a premium-quality, emotionally expressive 2D cartoon brain mascot character sheet for a professional Android digital wellbeing app named "BrainBuddy".

## MAIN CONCEPT

Design ONE adorable, living pink brain character that gradually becomes physically and emotionally exhausted from excessive short-video scrolling.

The character should initially look happy, energetic, lovable, and full of life.

As the user watches more Reels, Shorts, and TikToks, the SAME character gradually becomes tired, weak, pale, distressed, and nearly unable to stand.

The design should make users emotionally connect with their little brain and feel motivated to take a break.

## CHARACTER DESIGN

- Beautiful soft pink cartoon brain
- Expressive eyes with detailed pupils
- Natural eyebrows and small expressive mouth
- Tiny rounded arms and legs
- Soft, realistic-inspired brain folds
- Rounded organic silhouette
- Professional 2D illustrated mascot
- Soft gradients and gentle shading
- Subtle highlights and depth
- Mature, emotional cartoon style
- Visually attractive and memorable
- Premium mobile application illustration quality

IMPORTANT: Keep the cartoon style. Do NOT create a realistic anatomical brain.

## FOUR PROGRESSIVE POSES OF ONE CHARACTER

**POSE 1 — HAPPY AND ENERGETIC (0–24 Reels)**

- Bright healthy-looking pink brain
- Large sparkling eyes
- Genuine joyful smile
- Standing upright confidently
- Arms slightly raised
- Energetic posture
- Cheerful and lovable expression

**POSE 2 — STARTING TO FEEL TIRED (25–49 Reels)**

- EXACT SAME character
- Eyes slightly heavy and sleepy
- Smile beginning to disappear
- Slightly lowered eyebrows
- Arms hanging lower
- Slightly drooping posture
- Slightly faded pink color
- Subtle emotional fatigue

**POSE 3 — VERY WEAK AND EXHAUSTED (50–99 Reels)**

- EXACT SAME character
- Noticeable dark circles under eyes
- Heavy eyelids
- Sad, exhausted expression
- Slightly lowered head
- Arms hanging weakly
- Slouched body posture
- Pale pink-purple skin tone
- Small sweat drop
- Visibly struggling to remain energetic

**POSE 4 — EXTREMELY EXHAUSTED (100+ Reels)**

- EXACT SAME character
- STILL STANDING, but with great difficulty
- Head lowered significantly
- Eyes almost closed
- Pronounced tired dark circles
- Very weak and emotionally drained expression
- Tiny mouth showing exhaustion
- Both arms hanging loosely
- Slightly trembling legs
- Deeply slouched posture
- Dull pale pink-purple body
- Looks like it desperately needs rest
- Emotionally impactful and sympathetic

CRITICAL: The brain must NOT collapse onto the floor.

The final pose should look vulnerable, tired, and emotionally moving rather than funny.

## CHARACTER CONSISTENCY

All four poses MUST depict the EXACT SAME brain character.

Preserve:
- Identical underlying brain silhouette
- Identical brain fold patterns
- Same eyes and facial anatomy
- Same arm and leg design
- Same proportions
- Same illustration style
- Same viewing angle
- Same character identity

The character is progressively changing its emotional and physical appearance, NOT transforming into another character.

## ANIMATION-READY DESIGN

Design the mascot so an Android developer can animate:

- Natural blinking
- Opening and closing eyelids
- Subtle breathing
- Slight body bobbing
- Gradually lowering arms
- Changing facial expressions
- Slowly drooping posture
- Subtle exhausted trembling
- Smooth gradual color transitions

Keep all body parts clearly distinguishable for potential animation rigging.

## STRICTLY AVOID

- Overly childish kawaii style
- Silly comedy faces
- Tongue sticking out
- Exaggerated funny expressions
- Different brain characters
- Skeletons or skulls
- Horror, blood or gore
- Realistic medical brain anatomy
- Overcomplicated visual effects
- Text or typography
- Background scenery
- UI mockups

## IMAGE OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

- Four poses arranged horizontally
- ONE consistent character throughout
- Same scale and camera angle
- Front-facing three-quarter perspective
- Full body visible in every pose
- Generous spacing between poses
- No overlapping poses
- Transparent PNG background
- High resolution, preferably 4096 × 1024 or larger
- Sharp, clean edges
- Easy to crop into separate transparent assets
- Readable facial expressions even at small mobile overlay sizes
- Professional production-ready character sheet

## FINAL ART DIRECTION

A polished, emotionally expressive 2D cartoon mascot for a premium Android wellbeing application.

The same lovable pink brain slowly loses its energy and becomes visibly weaker with excessive scrolling.

The first pose should make users love the character.

The final pose should make users feel concerned for the same character and want to give it a break.

Beautiful, consistent, emotionally powerful, animation-friendly, minimal, and professional.

The visual changes symbolize scrolling fatigue and must not imply clinically measured brain damage.
